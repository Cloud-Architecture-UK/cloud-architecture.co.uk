Most of the hardening in this series works on **any** Microsoft 365 business or enterprise plan. A handful of controls need a premium identity tier or Defender/Purview capability, and each guide calls that out inline. This is the whole picture in one place.

Rule of thumb from the [licensing guide](/blog/microsoft-365-licensing-for-security/): **CIS Level 1 is achievable on Business Premium or E3**, and **Level 2 leans on E5** capabilities.

## The tiers that matter

- **Entra ID P1** - included with **Business Premium, E3 and E5**. Adds Conditional Access, group-based licensing, and the full authentication-methods / SSPR / banned-password experience.
- **Entra ID P2** - included with **E5** (or as an add-on). Adds PIM, risk-based Conditional Access and Identity Protection, and automated access reviews.
- **Defender for Office 365** - **Plan 1** (Safe Links / Safe Attachments) is bundled in **Business Premium** and an add-on for E3; **Plan 2** comes with **E5**.
- **Purview (advanced)** - auto-labelling, full DLP coverage, records management and long retention are **E5 / Purview** territory.
