This is the whole **Build & Secure Microsoft 365** series in the order I'd actually do it. Work top to bottom for a new tenant, or jump to the step you need. Each link is the full guide: the portal steps, the reasoning, and the PowerShell.

If you only read one thing first, read the [licensing map](/docs/licensing/) — it tells you which controls you can even turn on before you start.

## Foundations

Understand what you're hardening before you touch a setting.

<Steps>

1. [Set Up and Secure a Microsoft 365 Tenant (New or Existing)](/blog/setup-secure-microsoft-365-tenant-from-scratch/) — the map for everything below.
2. [What Microsoft Entra ID Actually Is](/blog/microsoft-entra-id-fundamentals-primer/) — the identity layer every later control sits on.
3. [Managing Users](/blog/microsoft-365-managing-users/) — joiners, and the leaver process that actually closes the hole.
4. [Groups and Group-Based Licensing](/blog/microsoft-365-groups-and-group-based-licensing/) — get this right early and every later step gets easier to target.
5. [Admin Roles and Least Privilege](/blog/microsoft-entra-admin-roles-and-rbac/) — the narrower Entra roles to hand out instead of Global Admin.

</Steps>

## The build — Steps 1 to 9

The tenant hardening itself, in sequence.

<Steps>

1. [Provisioning the Tenant](/blog/microsoft-365-tenant-provisioning-day-one/) — the first-ten-minutes decisions you can't take back.
2. [Break-Glass, Least Privilege and PIM](/blog/break-glass-accounts-least-privilege-pim/) — lock down standing admin access.
3. [The Conditional Access Baseline](/blog/conditional-access-baseline-every-tenant-needs/) — where Zero Trust stops being a slogan.
4. [Passwordless and Strong MFA](/blog/passwordless-mfa-microsoft-365/) — phishing-resistant sign-in, rolled out without stranding anyone.
5. [Securing Exchange Online](/blog/secure-exchange-online-anti-phishing-dmarc-defender/) — anti-phishing, the three email-auth records, and Defender.
6. [Data Protection with Purview](/blog/purview-sensitivity-labels-dlp-retention/) — a small, usable sensitivity-label and DLP starting point.
7. [App Consent and External Sharing](/blog/microsoft-365-app-consent-and-external-sharing/) — close the side doors MFA doesn't guard.
8. [Audit Logging, Alerts and Secure Score](/blog/microsoft-365-audit-logging-alerts-secure-score/) — make the tenant something you can see into.
9. [The Launch Checklist](/blog/microsoft-365-tenant-launch-checklist/) — work down it before you hand the tenant over.

</Steps>
