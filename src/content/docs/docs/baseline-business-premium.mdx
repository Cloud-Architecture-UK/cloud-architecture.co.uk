**Target: Microsoft 365 Business Premium** (Entra ID P1, Defender for Office 365 Plan 1). Achieves **CIS Level 1** and covers the five **Cyber Essentials** controls.

This is the whole hardening, distilled to one page: the controls in the order you apply them, what each one does, the standard it satisfies, and a link to the guide with the portal steps and the PowerShell. Work down it; tick each box; you have a defensible record of how the tenant was secured.

It's built from two things already on this site - the [framework map](/docs/framework-mapping/) (control to CIS/NIST) and the [licensing map](/docs/licensing/) (control to SKU). This baseline is the join: everything achievable on Business Premium, in sequence.

## The baseline

| # | Control | What to configure | CIS / NIST | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tenant provisioning | Org-wide defaults, restrict user consent and self-service tenant creation | Org settings · CM-6, AC-2, AC-6 | [Step 1](/blog/microsoft-365-tenant-provisioning-day-one/) |
| 2 | Break-glass admins | Two cloud-only emergency accounts, excluded from CA, alerted on sign-in | AC-2, CP-2 | [Step 2](/blog/break-glass-accounts-least-privilege-pim/) |

## Beyond Business Premium

These raise the bar but need **E5 or add-ons**, so they sit outside this baseline. The set above is complete and defensible on Business Premium alone; treat these as the upgrade path, not a gap.

- **PIM** (just-in-time admin) - Entra ID P2
- **Risk-based Conditional Access** and Identity Protection - Entra ID P2
- **Automatic labelling and full DLP** coverage - E5 / Purview
- **Automated access reviews** - Entra ID P2
- **Long audit retention and advanced hunting** - E5
